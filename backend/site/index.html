Download for Windows

Why Framewire

Beyond Discord's 60fps cap

Discord's screen share tops out at 60fps even on paid plans. Framewire streams 1440p/120fps or 1080p/144fps, so nothing gets lost for players on 144Hz/240Hz setups.

Built for a small group

Not 1-to-1 remote desktop, not a broadcast platform — up to 5 friends can watch the same P2P stream at once, joining with a 6-character room code.

Exclusive fullscreen, no compromise

Streams straight out of exclusive fullscreen for competitive players who won't switch to borderless, with a zero-copy GPU pipeline from capture to NVENC encode.

Requirements

Streaming host Windows 10 (1903+) / 11, NVIDIA GTX 1060 or newer (NVENC required), upload bandwidth ≈ 25 Mbps × number of viewers
Viewer Latest Chrome / Edge with hardware decode enabled — no install required
